Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Radio Premium is a radio player made by Michał Wójtowicz. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your favorite stations, settings (quality limit, ad volume, country filter) and the cached station list. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Services the app talks to

To play radio the app connects directly to these services. Like any internet connection, they see your device's IP address. Each service's own privacy policy applies.

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): the station directory. The app downloads station lists for the country you choose and sends your search text when you search all countries.
  • Radio stations' streaming servers: the audio comes straight from the station you play.
  • iTunes Search API (Apple): when “Look up cover art” is on, the artist and title of the song on air are sent to find cover art. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Maps: Apple Maps on iPhone, iPad and Mac; Google Maps on Android, used only when you open the Map tab.
  • AirPlay and Google Cast: the app looks for speakers on your local network only when you use these features. Audio sent to a Cast device is fetched by that device from the station.

The app does not request your location; the default country comes from your device's time zone and region settings, which are read on the device.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
