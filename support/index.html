Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see the song title or cover for some stations?
Only stations that broadcast song information can show it. Many public broadcasters don't send song titles in their streams, so the app shows the station instead and says so on the player screen.

How does the quality setting work?
Settings → Streaming quality sets a maximum. The app plays the best stream a station offers within that limit, or its lowest-quality stream if none fits.

What does “Lower volume during ads” do?
When a station marks an ad break in its stream, the app fades its own volume down by the amount you choose and back up afterwards. It works only on stations that announce ad breaks.

A station doesn't play.
Stations come from the community-run Radio Browser directory and sometimes go offline or change address. The app tries every stream a station offers. You can refresh the list in Settings → Station directory.

Which countries are included?
All countries in the Radio Browser directory. Use the country button on the Stations or Map screen, or choose “All countries”.
